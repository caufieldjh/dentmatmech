OHD:0000156 · predominantly base metal dental restoration material Metal In progress FDA Class II · 21 CFR 872.3710

A metal dental restoration material that is composed of group of cast metals that rely on Chromium for corrosion resistance.

Adverse effects and safety

Nickel and cobalt hypersensitivity Allergic or hypersensitivity

Nickel and cobalt have relatively high potential to cause allergy; the true risk from alloys containing them remains undefined.

Term
allergic contact reaction MONDO:0006525
1 evidence item
PMID:10668036 · Biocompatibility of dental casting alloys: a review. · Supports Systematic review or meta-analysis
“Several elements such as nickel and cobalt have relatively high potential to cause allergy, but the true risk of using alloys containing these elements remains undefined.”
Names nickel and cobalt as the sensitizers.

Beryllium and cadmium content in some alloys Toxicity

Known carcinogens in some chemical forms; carcinogenic effects from dental alloys have not been demonstrated but prudence argues for avoiding them.

1 evidence item
PMID:10668036 · Biocompatibility of dental casting alloys: a review. · Supports Systematic review or meta-analysis
“Several elements in casting alloys are known mutagens, and a few such as beryllium and cadmium are known carcinogens in different chemical forms.”
Identifies beryllium and cadmium.

A base metal alloy is a device composed primarily of base metals, such as nickel, chromium, or cobalt, that is intended for use in fabrication of cast or porcelain-fused-to-metal crown and bridge restorations.

Permitted uses

  • Fabrication of cast crown and bridge restorations Indirect restoration
  • Fabrication of porcelain-fused-to-metal crown and bridge restorations Indirect restoration
