Calcium hydroxide cement

OHD:0001045 · calcium hydroxide cement dental restoration material Cement In progress FDA Class II · 21 CFR 872.3250

A dental restoration material that contains calcium hydroxide as a chief ingredient and is suspended in a solvent carrier with a thickening agent.

Adverse effects and safety

Cytotoxicity of soluble extracts Toxicity

Soluble material from a hard-setting calcium hydroxide formulation is highly cytotoxic to cultured cells in vitro.
