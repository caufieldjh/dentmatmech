Zinc oxide eugenol cement

OHD:0001007 · zinc oxide eugenol cement dental restoration material Cement In progress FDA Class I · 21 CFR 872.3275

A zinc-oxide cement dental restoration material containing eugenol as a liquid component to complete the acid-base reaction.

Zinc oxide-eugenol is a device composed of zinc oxide-eugenol intended to serve as a temporary tooth filling or as a base cement to affix a temporary tooth filling, to affix dental devices such as crowns or bridges, or to be applied to a tooth to protect the tooth pulp.

Permitted uses

  • Temporary tooth filling Temporary
  • Base cement to affix a temporary tooth filling Liner or base
  • Affixing dental devices such as crowns or bridges Luting
  • Application to a tooth to protect the pulp Liner or base
