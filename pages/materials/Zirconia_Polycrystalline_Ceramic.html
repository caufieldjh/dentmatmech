Zirconia polycrystalline ceramic

OHD:0001034 · zirconia polycrystalline ceramic dental restoration material Ceramic In progress FDA

A polycrystalline ceramic dental restoration material that contains Zirconia which is partially stabilized zirconium oxide usually in the tetragonal phase.
