Noble metal

A metal dental restoration material that is composed of a metallic alloy that contains 25% or more (by weight) of noble metal elements.

Adverse effects and safety

Element release through corrosion Toxicity

Corrosion is the single most relevant property for biologic safety; noble and high-noble single-phase alloys release the least.

2 evidence items

A noble metal alloy is a device composed primarily of noble metals, such as gold, palladium, platinum, or silver, that is intended for use in the fabrication of cast or porcelain-fused-to-metal crown and bridge restorations.

Permitted uses

  • Fabrication of cast crown and bridge restorations Indirect restoration
  • Fabrication of porcelain-fused-to-metal crown and bridge restorations Indirect restoration

Product code EJT is gold-based noble metal alloy; EJS is other noble metal alloy.
